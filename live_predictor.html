Live Risk Predictor / SIH26153

Enter (or drag-to-simulate) current traffic-window stats and get a real-time risk score from the trained RandomForest — same model family as the forecasting pipeline, exported to run client-side. This is the single-window classifier (is this traffic suspicious), a companion to the sequence-based forecaster (will an attack occur in the next 6 windows), which needs rolling history and isn't drivable by manual single-point input.

Note: this classifier was trained only on real DDoS-labeled traffic (one CICIDS2017 day) — the "Normal traffic" and "DDoS flood" presets are genuine examples pulled directly from that labeled data. The "Moderate traffic" preset is illustrative only, since no real port-scan examples exist in this particular day's training data.
